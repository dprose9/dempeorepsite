God’s plan is for you


Heavenly Father is the author of the plan.
He is the Father of your spirit, and He loves you.
He wants to give you His greatest blessing—eternal
life with Him.  In other words, He wants you to become
more like Him and live the joyful, glorious life He lives.
He has all power and knows all things.  You can trust Him,
even when life is hard.
